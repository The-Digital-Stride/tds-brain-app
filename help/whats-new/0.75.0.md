The tour now walks you round the newer parts of Cortex: Projects, Working on, the Workbench, Images and Documents, and your Home cards.

It shows once on Home after this update. Press **Next** to move on, or **Skip tour** to leave. You can take it again any time from Help with **Take the tour**.

When Cortex gains a new app or feature, the tour shows again with the new parts in it.
