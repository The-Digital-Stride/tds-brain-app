## What it does

The **Asked** tab in the Tasks drawer lists the tasks you passed on to a teammate, or added for them. Each one shows who has it and since when. Finished ones show who ticked them off and when.

## Why it helps

- You can see what is still waiting without asking around.
- Finished jobs stay in view for 60 days, so you can see that they got done.
- You can open any of them to read the details in full.

## How to use it

1. Open the Tasks drawer and press **Asked**. The number is how many are still waiting.
2. **Waiting** shows what hasn't been done yet, longest-waiting first. **Done in the last 60 days** starts closed.
3. Press a task's title to read it in the panel. It is read only: it belongs to someone else now.
4. Any files added to it are listed under its details, in **Files**. You can open them, but not change them.
5. Open **History** at the bottom of the panel to see how it got here. It shows who added it, who it was passed to, and when it changed. Personal tasks have none.

## Good to know

- If you passed a task to Ben and he passed it on, only the latest hand-over counts, so it leaves your list.
- Some names come from git history (the Brain's sync record) rather than from the task itself. They are whoever's Mac synced the change, which may not be who made it. These are marked with a star in History, and as "(git history)" elsewhere.
- If the git history can't be read, the tab says so and lists only tasks passed on in the app or by Claude.
- Personal tasks aren't synced, so they have no history and never appear here.

## Try this

Pass a task on, then open **Asked** to see it waiting.
