title: Move a project to another client
pages: [projects]
---

Press **Move** on a project to give it to another client. You can also move a personal project to a client. Its open to-dos and files go with it.

Before the move, Cortex lists the files that will be shared. A project can't be moved back to personal.

The **New project** form now asks **Who is it for**: a client, or just you.
