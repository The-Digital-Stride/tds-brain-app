## What it does

Only owners and scouts see this. Ask one if you need it. **What's changed** in Settings (on the **About** tab) lists every version of Cortex, newest first, with what changed in each.

## Why it helps

- You can tell the team what is new.
- You can check when a fix arrived.
- It shows which version you have.

## How to use it

1. Open Settings from the rail, then the **About** tab.
2. Scroll down to **What's changed**.
3. Press a version to open it. The newest is open already.
4. Press **Show older versions** to see more.

## Good to know

- Each change has a label. **New** is a feature, **Fixed** is a repair, **Faster** is a speed-up and **Improved** is a tidy-up.
- Your own version says "(this version)".
- If the list can't load, Cortex says so. Try again later.
- It lists the versions your copy knows about. A newer one appears after you update.

## Try this

Open **What's changed** and find the version you are on.
