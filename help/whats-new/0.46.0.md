If you look after Google Ads, there's a new **Workbench** item on the left. It opens a list of tools. The first is **Ad Manager**.

Ad Manager does what the Ads tab in the Brain's Workbench does, without needing the Workbench running. You can approve, reject or ask for changes to proposals, apply approved ones, sign off monthly reports and see the ads board.

Changes to Google Ads always ask you to confirm first. The rail and Home show how many proposals are waiting for you.
