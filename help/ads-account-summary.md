## What it does

Ads Account Summary shows one client's Google Ads. At the top, **Headline figures** give spend, conversions, conversion value, ROAS and CPA for the last 7, 30 or 90 days, against the days before. Below, **Worth a look** lists what stands out. Below it are the data tables: campaigns, search terms, keywords and more.

## Why it helps

- The problems come first, each with a word that says how urgent it is.
- One press takes you to the table that holds them.
- Your table layout is kept for next time.

## How to use it

1. Open **Workbench**, then **Ads**, and choose **Ads Account Summary**.
2. Read **Worth a look**. **Act** is urgent, **Watch** is worth checking. Press the button on a line to jump to its table.
3. Press a column heading to sort a table. Press it again to reverse it.
4. To move a table, drag its grip. With the keyboard, focus the grip and use the arrow keys. Home and End move it to the start or the end.
5. In **Headline figures**, choose **7 days**, **30 days** or **90 days**. Each figure says how it changed against the period before.
6. Press **Full width** on a table to stretch it across the page. Press it again to put it back.
7. Press **Reset layout** to return every table to its first place and size.

## Good to know

- Only the headline figures follow the switch. The tables stay at the last 30 days.
- Google counts a conversion on the date of the click, so the newest days fill in over the next week or so. A 7-day view often shows a drop that isn't real.
- **Wasted spend** sits apart: search terms with no conversions in the last 30 days, whatever you pick above.
- These figures are what Google Ads reports (the **Google basis**). The client's own records can differ, so check before quoting one to a client.
- The headline figures appear once the updated Ads skill has been copied into the Brain.
- A table that is missing from the account's data is left out.
- **Refresh** at the top reloads the account.
