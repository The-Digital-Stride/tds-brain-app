title: More kinds of Home shortcut
pages: [customise-home]

Home shortcuts can now open a place or a drawer, as well as a skill or a tool. Open **Customise Home**, press **Add a shortcut**, and pick **Projects**, **Images** or **Documents** under **Places**, or **Chats** or **Tasks** under **Drawers**.

You can still have 6 shortcuts. The shortcuts you already have stay as they are.
