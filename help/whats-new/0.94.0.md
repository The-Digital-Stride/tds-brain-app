title: Upload files, and see what type they are
pages: [documents, images, projects]
---

You can now add files straight from [Documents](help:documents) and [Images](help:images). Press **Upload**, pick up to 10 files, and they are saved in the client's library.

On a project's **Files** tab, **Upload** saves the files in the client's library and adds them to the project. See [Projects](help:projects).

Every file also has a small grey label showing its type, such as PDF, DOCX or PNG. A PDF uploaded from Images goes to Documents, and Cortex tells you.
