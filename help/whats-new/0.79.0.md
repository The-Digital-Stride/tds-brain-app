title: Save a chat to its project
pages: [saving-chats-to-projects]
---

You can now keep a short record of a chat on its project. Press **Save to project** at the top of a chat you started. Claude writes a summary: the objective, the outcome, key figures, documents and next steps.

You check and change every word before anything is saved, and only what you see is saved, never the chat itself. Afterwards you can turn the next steps into to-dos for the project.

A project's Overview lists recent chats of yours that haven't been saved yet, and the summaries show on its Updates tab for the whole team.
