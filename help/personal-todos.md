## What it does

A personal to-do is private. It stays on your Mac, and your teammates never see it. Team to-dos are marked **TDS**.

## Why it helps

- Private reminders stay private.
- Your own list and the team's list sit side by side in one drawer.
- Your team list stays clean.

## How to use it

1. Switch on **Let the app change to-dos** in Settings (**General** tab), if it is off.
2. Open the To-dos drawer and press **Add a to-do**.
3. Press **Keep it personal** (the padlock) before you press **Add**.
4. To see only these, pick **Personal** at the top of the list. Pick **All** to see everything again.
5. To change an old to-do, open it, press **Edit** and use **Where it's kept**. Pick **TDS** or **Personal**, then press **Save**.

## Good to know

- Personal to-dos never sync. They aren't backed up, so they are lost if this Mac is wiped.
- The Workbench and Reminders don't see them either.
- A personal to-do can only be yours. If you give it to someone else, it becomes a TDS to-do so they can see it.
- If you don't see the padlock, your copy of the Brain may not have the feature yet.
