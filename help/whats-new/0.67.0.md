title: See what changed on a project since you last looked
pages: [projects]
---

A project's **Overview** now starts with **Since you last looked**. It lists new notes and calls, status changes, to-dos added or done, and new files. The list stays until you press **Clear**.

**Coming up** shows the to-dos due in the next two weeks, and the project's end date.

What you have seen is kept on your Mac only.
