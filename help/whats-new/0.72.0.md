Home has a new **Meetings** card. It lists your meetings for the next two weeks, from your own calendar.

Press the calendar icon beside a meeting to open its invite. From 15 minutes before the start, **Join** opens the video call.

On a project's page, the next meeting has **Open invite** and **Join** too. The last meeting has **Open invite**.
