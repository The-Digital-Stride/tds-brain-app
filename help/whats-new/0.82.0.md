title: Favourites are now pins
pages: [favourites, pinned]

Favourites are now called pins, and the star is now a pin. Press the pin beside a chat, task, skill or file to keep it at the top of its list. Pinned items are in a **Pinned** section in each drawer, and on Home under headings that start with **Pinned**.

Nothing you starred before has gone. It is all pinned already.
