title: Recent cards on Home
pages: [home, customise-home]
---

Home now shows what's newest, without you pinning it first. **Recent tasks** lists the newest tasks. **Recent chats** lists the chats you worked in last. **Recent documents** and **Recent images** list the newest files across your clients.

Your own personal files and tasks stay off these cards. Pin one if you want it on Home.

Don't want them? Open **Customise Home** and switch any card off.
