Projects in Cortex

A project is one tidy place for a piece of work. Its to-dos, files, pictures and documents (and later its chats, notes, emails, meetings, monthly retainer reviews and time) all point at it, so you can find everything about it in one go.

The shape

Client, then project, then to-do

  • One-off projects have an end, like a website rebuild. Ongoing ones are retainers, like monthly Google Ads work.
  • Each project is a folder, for example clients/skyseal/projects/website-rebuild/, with a short card file called project.md.
  • The card holds the same details Monday does: Account Owner, team, service, dates, budget hours, a health light and a client-comms light.
  • A project's folder name never changes, even if you rename its title. That's why links to it never break.

Retainers

Retainers, month by month

A retainer is an ongoing project with a signed contract, one project per contract. H2O's SEO and PPC retainers are two projects, because their hours, measures and notice differ.

On the retainer's pageExample
Hours this month11 of 15 hours used, on track to finish at 16
KPIs against targetConversions 38 / 40, with a small trend line across the months
Contract stripRolling monthly since 14 Nov · 90 days' notice · review 14 Feb
WarningsReview due within 30 days · notice not recorded · hours past 90% · a KPI missed two months running

An agency-wide Retainers page shows every retainer for the month on one screen: hours against budget, KPIs, health and renewals coming up. Today's contract details in each client's notes become retainer projects with a check by their owner, so nothing is retyped.

When

What arrives when

Release 1

Projects app and project page, a team that fills itself in from to-dos, the Client → Project picker, to-dos with project and stage, images and documents, search, the tidy-up.

Release 2

Chats and call notes carry the project. Pages are saved in the project. Updates log, "since you last looked", your meetings, approved email entries, a weekly round-up. Claude asks "which project?".

Release 3

Retainers: contract terms and KPIs, a monthly review signed by the Account Owner, client reports, and the agency-wide Retainers page.

Release 4

Ads work tagged to a project. Closing a project writes a short summary and keeps the folder.

Phase 2

Time from a timer, from Claude chats and from activity suggestions, each one approved by the person. Reports by client, project, person and month. Retainer hours then come from here.

Monday stays our live system for now. Projects copy Monday's shape, so moving across later is a straight swap: Monday projects become cards, subitems become to-dos, and booked hours come in marked "from Tracket".

Words

A few words explained

Card
The small file in a project's folder that holds its details, like a label on a box.
Key
A project's fixed address, such as skyseal/website-rebuild. To-dos and, later, time entries use it to point at the project.
Brain helper
A small script inside the brain that makes every change. The app asks it to make changes and never edits brain files itself.
Sync
How the brain shares changes with the team. If two people change the same line before syncing, it stops until someone sorts it out. The plan avoids that by never stamping "last edited" into files.
Retainer
Ongoing monthly work under a signed contract, with a set number of hours a month and measures it's judged on.
KPI
A number that shows whether the work is going well, such as conversions or organic clicks, with a target to aim for.
Worked out, not stored
Cortex builds some lists (the "from to-dos" team, "since you last looked") fresh each time from what's already in the brain, so they can never go out of date.
Stage
An optional label that groups a project's to-dos, such as Build or Launch.
