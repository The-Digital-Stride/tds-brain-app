title: Keep a project to its own team
pages: [restricted-projects, projects]
---

A project can now be **restricted**. Open the project, press **Edit**, and under **Who can see it** choose **Only the project team**. Only its Account Owner, its team, and the owners and scouts can see it in Cortex. See [Restricted projects](help:restricted-projects).

It hides the project in Cortex only. The files are still in the Brain, so read what it does not do before you rely on it.
