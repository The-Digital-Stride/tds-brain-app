To-dos are now called tasks. Nothing else has changed: your tasks, who they're assigned to and their due dates are all where you left them. The team starts using the new word on Monday 30 November.

The rail button, the Tasks drawer, Home, projects and help all say tasks now, and Claude says it in chats too. If you type "to-do" in search out of habit, it still takes you to Tasks.
