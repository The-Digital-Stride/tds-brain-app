title: Chats open beside the project
pages: [project-chats, projects, picking-the-client]
---

Press **New chat for this project** and the chat now opens beside the project, not on Home. You stay where you were.

The message box shows **Project** and **Chat for**, both already filled in. When you send, the reply appears in the same place.

If you have typed something and go to leave, Cortex asks before your text is lost. **Open full screen** gives the chat more room and keeps your message.
