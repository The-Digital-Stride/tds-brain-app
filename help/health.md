## What it does

The top of Settings has a heading, **How TDS Brain is running**. Under it is one sentence, then a line for each thing Cortex needs.

## Why it helps

- You spot a problem before it stops your work.
- Every line says what is wrong in plain words.
- You know what to tell whoever looks after Cortex.

## How to use it

1. Open Settings from the rail.
2. Read the first sentence. It says if everything is connected and working.
3. If something is wrong, read the line marked **Needs attention**.
4. For the fix, see the Fixing problems page.

## Good to know

Each line means this:

- **Connection:** Cortex is live-linked to your Mac, so changes show as they happen.
- **Brain folder:** the Brain was found. **Change…** lets you pick another folder.
- **Claude Code:** Claude is ready to start chats.
- **Brain sync:** the Brain updated in the last 5 minutes. After that, it shows as stale.
- **To-dos and chats:** the lists have loaded.
- **Starts by itself:** Cortex starts when you log in. A **Note** means it was started by hand.
- **Sign-in:** you are signed in on this computer.

## Try this

Open Settings and check **Brain sync**. It should show a time from the last few minutes.
