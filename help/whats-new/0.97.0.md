title: See when a task changes
pages: [task-notices, task-updates, asked-of-others]
---

- Know when a teammate updates, finishes or hands back a task. A red number on **Tasks** on the rail counts them. See [Knowing when a task changes](help:task-notices).
- The Tasks drawer has an **Updated** section, on **Mine** and on **Asked**, that says what happened and when.
- Home has a new **Task updates** card.
- Open a task and the updates you haven't seen are marked **New**.
- A task you handed back now says so in your **Asked** list.
