title: Recent projects on Home
pages: [home, projects]
---

Home now has a **Recent projects** card. It lists your open projects, with the one worked on last at the top and the day it was last worked on. Press a row to open the project, or **All projects** to see them all.

The card shows once a project has been worked on. You can drag it to a new place like any other card.
