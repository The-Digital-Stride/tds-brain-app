The **Brain** is the agency's shared folder of knowledge. It holds client notes, files, tasks and the skills Claude can use. Everyone at TDS shares it.

**Cortex** is the app you use to reach it. You chat with Claude, read files and tick off tasks. Cortex does the work. The Brain remembers it.

## Why two names?

The Brain stays the same if the app changes. Cortex can get new features while your notes stay where they are. When someone says "it's in the Brain", they mean the shared folder. When they say "open Cortex", they mean this app.

## How they work together

- You ask Claude for something in Cortex.
- Claude reads the Brain to learn the background.
- When Claude files something, it goes into the Brain.
- Your teammates see it the next time their Brain syncs. A sync is when your computer's copy of the Brain catches up with the shared one.

## What you will see

The rail shows a **Synced** stamp near the bottom. It tells you when your Brain last synced. If it turns red, your Brain hasn't synced for more than five minutes. See [How Cortex is running](help:health).

## A note on names

TDS Brain is now TDS Cortex. That is the same app with a new name. You may still see the old name in a few places. The sign-in helper keeps its name, TDS Brain Sign-in. See [Signing in](help:signing-in).
