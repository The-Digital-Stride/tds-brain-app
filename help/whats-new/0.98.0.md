title: See and confirm your day
pages: [my-day]
---

- Owners and scouts have a new **My day** page. It builds your day from your chats, so you can see where the time went. See [My day](help:my-day).
- Change any entry, add one Cortex missed, then press **Confirm day** to send it to Toolbox.
- Nothing is sent until you confirm, and a failed send keeps your day on this Mac.
