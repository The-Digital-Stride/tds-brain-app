## What it does

Cortex checks for a newer version a few minutes after it starts, then about every six hours. A copy you started by hand doesn't check by itself. When one is ready, a bar tells you, and you choose when to update.

## Why it helps

- You get fixes and new features without any setup.
- You choose the moment, so it never interrupts your work.
- If an update fails, Cortex usually stays on the version you had. If it can't, press **Try again**.

## How to use it

1. When the bar says a new version is available, press **Update and restart**.
2. If chats are open, Cortex warns you that updating closes them. Press **Update now**, or **Not now** to wait.
3. The foot of the menu on the left shows your version. When an update is ready, press **Update** there. Otherwise, press the **Check for updates** button beside it to check now.
4. You can also check in Settings: open Settings, then the **About** tab. Under **About TDS Cortex on this computer**, find **App version** and press **Check for updates**.
5. If you're busy, press **Later** on the bar.
6. After an update, a What's new card may show on Home with a short note. Press **Close** to hide it.

## Good to know

- Updating restarts Cortex. Your chats close, so finish anything important first.
- If an update stops part-way, press **Try again** to finish it.
- Some copies can't update themselves, such as one started by hand. Settings says why. Ask whoever looks after Cortex.
- Everyone can read what changed in each version. See Version history.

Press **Check for updates** at the foot of the menu. It tells you if you have the newest version.
