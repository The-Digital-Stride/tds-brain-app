## What it does

Projects shows the team's work for each client: each project's brief, team, tasks and files.

## Why it helps

- You see all of a client's projects together.
- Finished projects leave the list but stay safe.

## How to use it

1. Open Projects.
2. Choose **Mine**, or filter by status, Account Owner and service.
3. Open a project. **Overview** shows what's new, what's due, the brief and team. **Tasks** lists everyone's; choose **Mine** there to see just yours. **Updates** logs notes, calls, emails, [chat summaries](help:saving-chats-to-projects) and changes. Notes stay internal unless you tick **Fine to share with the client**. **Files** lists documents and images. **Add files** adds library files; the cross removes one. **Add a claude.ai page** saves one.
4. **New project** adds one; **Edit** changes one. **Add to team** keeps a person marked "from tasks".
5. **New chat for this project** starts a chat that saves its files here.
6. **Move** moves a project to another client.
7. When a project is finished, press **Archive** and choose what happens to each open task.
8. Find archived projects under the **Archived** filter. **Unarchive** brings one back.
9. On a tidy-up notice, press **Tidy up**, then check each label.
10. To remove an archived project, press **Delete** and type its name.

## Good to know

- **Since you last looked** keeps its changes until you press **Clear**.
- **Meetings** shows your last and next meeting, on your Mac only. **File the notes** files them.
- A client project's **Overview** shows key facts from the client's business file. It's changed in the brain, not here.
- A project without a card is read-only until you press **Add a card**.
- Anyone who can edit a project can archive it. Only its Account Owner or the brain's Owner can delete it.
- Anyone on the team can edit shared projects. Only the Account Owner, an owner or a scout can change the Account Owner.
- Personal projects are only yours. A move can't make a project personal again.
- Delete is final for files only on this Mac. Deleted tasks can be restored; chats are kept.
- Tidy-up changes teammates' tasks, so one person runs it.
- Update Cortex on every Mac before archiving.
- Old brain skills make Projects read-only.
