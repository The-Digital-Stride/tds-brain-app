title: Ad Manager is now three tools under Ads
pages: [ad-manager, ads-command-centre, ads-account-summary]
---

**Ad Manager** is now three tools, grouped under **Ads** in **Workbench**. **Ads Manager** is for decisions: what needs you, the proposals waiting for approval, and the monthly close. **Ads Account Summary** is for reading an account: what's worth a look, then the report tables.

**Run**, the board and the daily standup have moved to **Ads Command Centre**, along with the check that your account data is ready. Each page shows the same account name at the top, and buttons on each take you to the other two.
