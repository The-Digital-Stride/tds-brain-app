TDS Brain App: three looks

Each look is drawn twice: the start screen and a chat in progress. The content is the same in all three, so the only thing changing is the look. These are sketches to choose from, not finished screens.

Brand colours, typefaces and the logo mark are taken from the TDS website: navy #021B43, blue #1889FF, paper #FAF8F4, Poppins for headings and Inter for text.

In all three, whichever you pick

  • The message box sits directly under the greeting on the start screen, not at the bottom of an empty page.
  • The start screen shows what needs you today, so the to-do panel isn't needed there.
  • To-dos are rows, not cards. Clicking a row starts a chat on it; the "Start a chat on this" button on every item goes.
  • The to-do panel shows beside chats only. Skills, History and Settings get the full width.
  • Poppins and Inter replace the system font, and the TDS mark sits beside the name.
  • Small labels are written normally, not in capitals. Tick boxes get bigger.
1

House style

The app as a TDS product: the navy side menu, paper background and blue action from the website. Closest to what you have now, so the least change for anyone already using it.

2

Paper desk

Light all the way across. Navy is kept for text and the one main button, so the status colours (ready, asking, working) are the only bright things on screen. The calmest of the three for a long day in it.

3

Stride

The whole frame is navy and the work sits on one paper sheet in the middle. The start screen leads with your week as a row of leaning bars, taken from the six bars of the TDS mark: overdue, today, tomorrow, later. The boldest and the most recognisably TDS.
