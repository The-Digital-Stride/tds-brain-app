## What it does

Chats stay on your own Mac. **Save to project** lets Claude write a short summary of a chat, so the whole team can read it under the project's **Updates**.

## Why it helps

- The team sees what was done and how it ended.
- You write nothing from scratch. You only check and change Claude's draft.
- The next steps can become real tasks in one go.

## How to use it

1. In a chat, press **Save to project**. If the chat has no project yet, choose one.
2. Wait while Claude writes the summary. Press **Cancel** to stop.
3. Check the objective, outcome, key figures, documents and next steps. Change anything you like.
4. Tick **Fine to share with the client** only if the client may read it.
5. Press **Save to project**. Nothing is saved before this.
6. To make tasks, tick the next steps you want, choose who gets each one, and press **Add tasks**. Press **Done** to finish.

On a project's **Overview**, **Chats not saved yet** lists your chats from the last 30 days that have new messages. Press **Save to project** beside one. The [**Chats** tab](help:project-chat-list) lists every chat, saved or not.

## Good to know

- Only you can save your own chats. Nobody else can save them for you.
- Saving the same chat again covers only what happened since last time. Older summaries stay as they were.
- If the chat used your email, the form says so. Saving it is your OK for exactly the text you see.
- A file from your **personal** folder can't be linked on a shared project.
- To remove a summary, press the bin beside it on the **Updates** tab.

## Try this

Finish a chat about a client job, then press **Save to project** and read the draft.
