title: Passed-on tasks stay in Asked
pages: [asked-of-others, task-notices]
---

A task you asked someone to do now stays in your **Asked** list when they pass it on to someone else. It shows who has it now and since when, and the pass-on counts as an update. Before, it left your list without a word. See [Asked of others](help:asked-of-others).
