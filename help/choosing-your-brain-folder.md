## What it does

Cortex needs the folder where you keep your copy of the Agency Brain. A screen called **Choose your brain folder** lets you pick it.

## Why it helps

- Cortex reads your skills, to-dos and client notes from there.
- You choose the folder once, and it remembers.
- It lists the Brains it finds on your Mac, so you rarely type a path.

## How to use it

1. If Cortex can't find the Brain, it shows this screen by itself. You can also press **Change…** next to **Brain folder** in Settings.
2. Look under **Found on this Mac**. The one in use says **In use now**.
3. Press **Use this folder and restart** next to the right one. (A copy you started by hand says **Use this folder**.)
4. If it isn't listed, paste the path into **Folder path**. In Finder, select the folder and press Option-Command-C to copy its path.
5. Press **Use this folder and restart**.

## Good to know

- If Cortex starts when you log in, it restarts after you choose. That closes any open chats, and Cortex warns you first.
- If it can't restart, it says "Stop TDS Cortex and start it again".
- You need the Agency Brain on your Mac first. If you don't have it, ask whoever looks after Cortex.
- Press **Check again** if Cortex hasn't come back after a minute.

## Try this

Open Settings and check the path under **Brain folder**. Is it the folder you expect?
