version: 0.87.1
title: Skills start from chats
pages: [skills, customise-home]
---

Home shortcuts no longer start a skill. They open a place, a drawer or a tool. If you had a skill shortcut, it has gone. Your other shortcuts stay as they are.

Only owners and scouts now see the **Pinned skills** card on Home and skills in **Search or jump to**. Everyone can still start a skill by typing a slash and its name in a chat, such as `/meeting-notes`. Buttons made for one skill, such as **Start my day**, work as before.
