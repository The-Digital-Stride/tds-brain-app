Restricted projects

Some projects are private. This page shows how Cortex will keep them out of sight, and, just as important, what it will not do.

1 · The short answer

One switch hides a project

A project can be marked Restricted. Cortex then shows it only to its Account Owner, its team, and the agency's owners and scouts.

Cortex is the TDS Brain App. The Account Owner is the person who looks after that client. The team is the people listed on the project. Owners and scouts are the people who run the agency. Everyone else will not see the project at all.

3 · How the hiding works

One gate, not fifteen

Cortex could hide a project by teaching every screen to hide it. That is fifteen places to get wrong. Instead there is one checkpoint. The list that every screen reads from simply never contains a hidden project.

4 · Tasks

You can't hand a hidden project's task to someone who can't see it

5 · The switch

Three kinds of person can switch it on or off

6 · What this does not do

It hides the project. It does not lock it.

This is the risky part, so it is spelled out here: Restricted is a curtain, not a lock. Cortex hides the project. The files are still there.

The files are still on every Mac

The project's files stay in the brain folder, and that folder is copied to every team Mac. Someone who opens it outside Cortex, in Finder or in the terminal (the typing window for computer commands), could still read them.

An older Cortex still shows it

Hiding only works on a Mac that has the new version of Cortex. Anyone on an older version still sees the project until they update.

The chat assistant is a guard rail, not a wall

If you ask the chat assistant to open a hidden project's folder, it says no. But a broad search across the whole brain, or a brain skill run in chat, could still turn up a line from the project's files.

A real lock, where the files are not on other people's Macs at all, is a later piece of work.

7 · What happens when

Nothing changes until someone flips the switch

TodayEvery project stays open to everyone, just as it is now.
Someone switches one onCortex adds one line to that project's card file. Switching it off takes the line away.
The filesNothing is moved or renamed. The folder stays exactly where it is.
A muddled cardIf Cortex can't tell, it hides the project instead of showing it.
