title: Meetings on a project's page
pages: [projects]
---

A project's **Overview** now shows your last and next meeting for it. They come from your own calendar, via /gm, on your Mac only.

After a meeting, press **File the notes** to start a chat that files them. Once the notes are filed, the meeting says so, with a link to open them.
