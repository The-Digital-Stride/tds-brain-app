Ask Claude for an email in a project chat and the draft now shows as a card. The card lists who it goes to, the subject and the full text.

Press **Open in Gmail** and a small Gmail window opens with it all filled in. Check it there, then send it. Nothing is sent until you press Send.

**Copy text** copies the email, for pasting into a reply in an older thread.
