Start in Settings, on the **Health** tab. It says what's wrong. A red dot on the tab shows when something needs attention. [How Cortex is running](help:health) explains each line.

## Changes in the Brain don't show up

The panel says "Lost the live connection". Wait a minute. If it lasts longer, restart your Mac or tell whoever looks after Cortex.

## The Brain hasn't synced

The panel says "Not synced since" a time. Check you're online. Check Brain Connect (the app that syncs the Brain) is running. It normally syncs every minute.

## To-dos or chats won't load

Press Command and R to try again.

## Claude Code isn't ready

Chats can't start. The panel says why. Tell whoever looks after Cortex.

## A file won't attach

The tag says **Can't attach**. The file may be too big, empty or the wrong type. Remove it, then send. See [Attaching files](help:attaching-files).

## A chat stopped

It says "Stopped by a problem". Send your message again.

## I can't start another chat

You can have four chats open at once. If all four are busy, wait, or close one.

## Still stuck

Ask someone at TDS who looks after Cortex.
