## What it does

Ad Manager shows the changes Claude has proposed for a client's Google Ads account. You read each one, then approve it, ask for changes or reject it. Below the proposals is the account's ads board: how healthy its data is, jobs you can start, and tables of campaigns, search terms and keywords.

Only owners and scouts can use it. Ask one if you need it.

## Why it helps

- You see what waits for you, client by client.
- Every live change needs your yes, and a second press before it happens.
- Starting a routine job is one press, and Claude does the work in a new chat.

## How to use it

1. Open **Workbench** on the rail, then **Ad Manager**.
2. Pick a client in **Account**. A number in brackets shows how many proposals wait for you.
3. Under **Waiting for a decision**, press **Details** on a proposal. Its facts open in the panel on the right.
4. Choose **Approve**, **Request changes** or **Reject**. Rejecting and requesting changes both need a note.
5. For an approved change, try **Dry run** first. It checks everything and changes nothing. Then press **Apply now** to make it live.
6. Press **Run** on any card on the board to start a chat for that job.

## Good to know

- Cortex asks you to confirm approving, rejecting, applying and signing off a report.
- If Cortex can't tell whether an apply reached Google Ads, it says so and stops. Check the account, then press **I've checked the account**.
- **Recent decisions** covers the last 14 days.
- Use **Refresh** if you think the screen is behind.
- **Rate calls** lets you score the Daily standup's advice.

## Try this

Open Ad Manager, press **Details** on the first proposal, and read it without deciding.
