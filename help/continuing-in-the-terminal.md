Only owners and scouts see this. Ask one if you need it.

## What it does

Every Cortex chat can be carried on in the terminal (the Mac app where you type commands to Claude). The **This chat** panel gives you the line to type.

## Why it helps

- You can use the terminal's own tools on the same job.
- Claude keeps everything said so far.

## How to use it

1. Open the [This chat panel](help:this-chat-panel) and choose **Details**.
2. Find **Continue in the terminal**. Copy the line that starts with `claude --resume`.
3. Open the terminal in the Brain folder.
4. Paste the line and press Enter.

## Good to know

- Chats started in Cortex don't show in the terminal's own list. Use the line.
- The line only appears once Claude has started the chat.
- **Approvals** says "Same as your terminal", so Cortex asks as often as your terminal does.
- Deleting an earlier chat also removes it from the terminal. See [Deleting a chat](help:deleting-a-chat).

## Try this

Copy the line from a finished chat, paste it in the terminal, and ask Claude to summarise it.
