title: Save a message for later
pages: [project-chats, projects]
---

Halfway through a message beside a project, and need to check something else? When Cortex asks about your text, press **Save for later**.

Your words, the project and the client are kept. The button you started from changes its name to **Open saved message**. Press it and your message is back in the box, ready to finish.

A saved message stays until you send it, discard it or clear the box. It is kept in this browser, for you only. Attached files can't be saved, so send or remove those first.
