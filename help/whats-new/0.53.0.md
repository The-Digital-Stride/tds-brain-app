title: Tabs for the Ads tools, and a menu that shows its names when you point at it
pages: [workbench, ad-manager]
---

The three Ads tools now sit as **tabs** at the top of the page: **Manager**, **Command Centre** and **Account Summary**. Press a tab to switch. The client you chose stays the same, and so does any task chat you have open. The numbers from the Workbench list show on the tabs too.

The two buttons in **Ads Manager** that opened the other tools have gone. Use the tabs instead.

The menu on the left now slides out its names when you point at it. Move the pointer away and it slides back.
