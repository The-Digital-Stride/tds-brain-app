## What it does

Ask Claude for an email in a project chat and the draft shows as a card. The card lists who it goes to, the subject and the full text. Press **Open in Gmail** and a small Gmail window opens with all of it filled in.

## Why it helps

- You check and send the email in Gmail, as you always do.
- The card shows exactly what Gmail will get, so there are no surprises.

## How to use it

1. Open a chat for a project. On the project's page, press **New chat for this project**.
2. Ask for the email. Say who it is for: "Draft an email to Jo about the quote."
3. Read the card. Ask Claude for changes, and it writes the email again.
4. Press **Open in Gmail**. Check it there, then press Send.

## Good to know

- Nothing is sent until you press Send in Gmail. If you close the window, Gmail may keep a copy in Drafts. Delete it there if you don't want it.
- The email is plain text. Add bold or links in Gmail if you need them.
- Your team signature is added when the email is sent, so the draft has none.
- If the project has a code, the draft ends with it. Later replies then find the project.
- Claude only fills in an address you gave it, or one from the client's files. If the card says **Nobody yet**, add the person in Gmail.
- A reply to an older email opens as a new email. Press **Copy text** and paste it into the thread in Gmail.
- A very long email is too big to fill in. The card tells you, and **Copy text** gets it across.
- Hold Cmd as you click to open Gmail in a tab, not a small window.

## Try this

In a project chat, ask Claude to draft a short email to the client's main contact.
