pages: [ad-manager, ads-command-centre, ads-account-summary]

**Ads Command Centre** has **All accounts' standups…** on the Daily standup card, so you can see which client needs you most and jump to it. A task that saved a report now shows **Open result** on its card, so you can read what a teammate ran. The Daily standup doesn't get one.

**Ads Account Summary** opens with headline figures for 7, 30 or 90 days, against the days before. A 7-day view can show a drop that isn't real, because Google counts a conversion on the click's date. **Ads Manager** lists **Earlier months** and has **Record and draft the change**.

These appear once the updated Ads skill is copied into the Brain.
