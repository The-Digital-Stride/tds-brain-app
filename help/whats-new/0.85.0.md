title: Make Home your own
pages: [customise-home]
---

You can now choose what shows on Home. Press the panel icon at the top right of Home to open **Customise Home**. Switch off the cards you don't use, and add shortcut buttons for the skills and tools you use most.

Nothing is hidden until you choose. Every card is still on Home. If your last card sat on its own, it now fills the row.
