## What it does

The **Save it** button keeps your work safe. You press it once and Claude does the rest. It works on every chat you open, so nothing gets lost along the way.

## Why it helps

- You never lose a draft, even when you close the window by mistake.
- Your teammates can see what you saved, so nobody repeats the same work.
- You spend less time worrying and more time getting jobs done.

## How to use it

1. Open the chat you want to keep.
2. Press **Save it** at the top of the chat.
3. Wait for the small tick to show that the save worked.

![The chat with a Save it button](images/good.webp)

## Good to know

- Saving takes a second or two on a slow connection.
- Anyone on the team can open a saved chat from the list.

## Try this

Open a chat from yesterday and save it now, then find it again in the list tomorrow.
