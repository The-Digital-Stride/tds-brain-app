Claude can now suggest client emails for a project. Each one waits for the person whose inbox it came from.

Open **Projects** and look under **Waiting for your OK**. Press **Review**, check the summary, then press **Save to project**. The team then sees it under the project's **Updates**.

Only the summary, subject, names and date are saved. The email itself never is.
