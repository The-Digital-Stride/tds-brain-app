## What it does

A level is how hard Claude works on a chat. There are three. **Quick** is for look-ups, short answers and simple edits. **Standard** is for most writing and reports. **Deep** is for audits, strategy and anything that needs careful judgement.

## Why it helps

- Quick jobs don't wait on a heavy level.
- Hard jobs get the most careful one.
- You don't need to know about models or effort. Cortex picks for you.

## How to use it

1. Send your message. Under the message box, a line shows the level, such as "Standard · picked by the app for this chat".
2. Press **Why?** to see the reason. It also names the model and effort used.
3. To change it, press **Change** and choose a level. It stays for the rest of that chat.
4. To let Cortex choose again, press **Change**, then **Back to automatic**.

![The level line in a chat, showing the level picked for this chat.](images/level-line.webp)

## Good to know

- Quick uses Haiku, Standard uses Sonnet and Deep uses Opus. These are different Claude models. Effort is how much thinking Claude does.
- To choose the model and effort yourself, open Settings, then the **Claude** tab. Under **Claude in chats**, set **Level** to **Choose myself**. The **Model** and **Effort** lists then appear.
- Some skills have a level set by the team. The line then says "team level". See [Skill levels](help:skill-levels).
- Cortex sometimes stays on a higher level mid-chat, because switching down would cost more.
