title: Ad Manager shows who ran each job
pages: [ad-manager]

Each job in **Ad Manager** now shows when it last ran, and who ran it if it was a teammate. While a job's chat is still going, the card says **Running** and its button opens that chat.

The report tables sit two to a row. Drag a table by its grip to move it, or make it full width. Your layout stays on this Mac.
