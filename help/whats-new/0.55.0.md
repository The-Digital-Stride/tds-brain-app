**Projects** is new in the rail. Each project shows its brief, its team, its to-dos and its files on one page.

Choose **Mine** to see the projects you are on. You can also filter by status, Account Owner and service. Press **New project** to add one.

To-dos can now belong to a project, and the team is worked out from who has its to-dos. The client pickers on Home, Documents, Images and search let you pick a project too.
