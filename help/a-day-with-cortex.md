Here is how one day could go. You don't have to do all of it. Pick the parts that match your work.

## Morning: see what needs you

Open Cortex. Home greets you and sums up what needs attention. Your favourite tasks, chats and files sit underneath. See [Favourite tasks, chats and files](help:pinned).

## After a call: file it

Paste your notes into a chat and type "File these under Northwind." Claude files them in the Brain and tells you where they went. Your teammates can find them too. Nobody has to set up access.

## Mid-day: a client report

Ask for a draft report for Acme Heating. Claude uses what the Brain knows about the client. You read it, ask for changes and send it on.

## Afternoon: pages and content

Ask Claude for the first version of a page or a piece of content. Claude can show it as a page in the chat, so you read it the way a visitor would. See [Pages Claude makes](help:pages-claude-makes).

## Before you leave: hand things over

Add a task for a teammate, or pass one on. It shows up for them the next time their Brain syncs, and they see it is new to them. The rail's **Synced** stamp tells you when your own Brain last synced. See [Sharing with your team](help:sharing-with-your-team).

## Then what?

Tomorrow, Claude still knows what you filed today. That is the point of the Brain, and Cortex is how you use it.
