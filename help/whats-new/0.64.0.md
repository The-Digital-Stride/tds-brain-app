title: Cortex asks before you lose typed work
pages: [projects]
---

If you have typed into a project form and try to go somewhere else, Cortex asks **Discard your changes?** first. Press **Keep editing** to go back to your work.

Health now reads in words: On track, At risk or Off track.

Projects and to-dos both use **Add files** to add files, and a cross to take one off.
