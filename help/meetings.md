## What it does

The **Meetings** card on Home lists your coming meetings from your own calendar, for the next two weeks. It shows every meeting, whether or not it belongs to a project.

## Why it helps

- You see all of today and what's next in one place.
- Each meeting links to its invite, so you can open it in Google Calendar.
- Near the start, a **Join** button opens the video call.

## How to use it

1. Open Home. The **Meetings** card is the first card under the message box.
2. Press the calendar icon beside a meeting to open its invite in Google Calendar. It opens in a new tab.
3. Press **Join** to open the video call. It shows from 15 minutes before the start.
4. Press a project's name to open that project.
5. Press the Show more button to see the rest.

## Good to know

- The card says when your calendar was last checked. If it's old, run /gm to update it.
- **Working on** doesn't narrow your meetings. You always see them all.
- If a meeting has no event page of its own, the calendar icon opens that day instead.
- On a project's page, the same link reads **Open invite**.
- Only you see your meetings. Nobody else's calendar is used.

## Try this

Open Home and press the calendar icon beside your next meeting.
