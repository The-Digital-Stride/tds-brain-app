The **Recent** cards on Home now show your own things, not the whole team's. **Recent tasks** lists tasks assigned to you. **Recent documents** and **Recent images** list files you changed. **Recent projects** lists the projects you worked on last.

A file Claude makes in your chat shows straight away, even before your Brain syncs.
