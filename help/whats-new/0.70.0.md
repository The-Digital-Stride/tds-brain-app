title: Keep claude.ai pages in a project
pages: [projects, pages-claude-makes]
---

A project's **Files** tab has a new button: **Add a claude.ai page**. Paste the page's link, and Cortex keeps it in the project.

Most pages are saved as a copy you can open in Cortex. A page that only works live on claude.ai is kept as a card with its link.

Either way, **Open on claude.ai** takes you to the page itself.
