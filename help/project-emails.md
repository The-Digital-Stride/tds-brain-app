## What it does

Claude can suggest client emails for a project. Each one waits for the person whose inbox it came from. Nothing is saved until they check it and press **Save to project**. Then the team sees it under the project's **Updates**.

## Why it helps

- Decisions, budgets and dates agreed by email reach the project, not just one inbox.
- You see exactly what will be saved, and you can change it first.

## How to use it

1. Run /gm, or ask in a chat: "link this email thread to the project".
2. Open **Projects**. Emails waiting for you are under **Waiting for your OK**, and on the project's **Overview**.
3. Press **Review**. Check the project, the summary and the names.
4. Press **Save to project**, or **Not for a project** to leave it out for good.

## Good to know

- Only the summary, subject, names and date are saved. The email itself and email addresses never are.
- Notes stay internal unless you tick **Client can see this**.
- Only you can save emails from your inbox. Teammates see "In Sam's inbox" instead of a Gmail link.
- A project code anywhere in the thread picks the project for you. Paste it once and later replies still find it. A code in a signature never counts.
- A conversation is one row on **Updates**. Each person adds only what is new. Open **Earlier in this thread** to read the older parts.
- Each part keeps its own **Client can see this** setting and its own delete button.
- Did a summary miss something? The person who saved that part deletes it and saves again.
- To start an email from a project, press **Write an email** on its **Overview**. Gmail opens in a small window with the code already in it.
- If the client has contacts with an email address, an **Email to** list sits beside the button. Pick who the email goes to, or **Someone else** to fill it in yourself.
- The list comes from the client's business file and profile in the brain. To add someone, add their name and email address under the contacts there.
