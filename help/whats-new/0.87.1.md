title: Skills start from chats
pages: [skills, customise-home]

Home shortcuts no longer start a skill. They open a place, a drawer or a tool. If you had a skill shortcut, it has gone. Your other shortcuts stay as they are.

Only owners and scouts now see the **Pinned skills** card on Home and skills in **Search or jump to**. Everyone can still ask Claude for a skill in a chat.
