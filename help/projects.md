## What it does

Projects shows the work the team does for each client. Each project has a page with its brief, team, to-dos and files. Two lights show how it is going and how the client feels.

## Why it helps

- You see all of a client's projects together.
- Finished projects leave the list but are never lost.

## How to use it

1. Open Projects from the rail.
2. Choose **Mine** to see only your projects. Use the filters for status, Account Owner and service.
3. Open a project. **Overview** shows the details and team. **To-dos** shows what is open. **Files** shows images and documents. Press **Link files** to link several at once.
4. Press **New project** to add one. Press **Edit** to change one.
5. To move a project to another client, press **Move…**. You see what will happen first.
6. When a project is finished, press **Archive…** on its Overview. Choose what happens to each open to-do, then press **Archive**.
7. To see archived projects, set the status filter to **Archived**. Open one and press **Unarchive** to bring it back.
8. If you see a tidy-up notice, press **Tidy up** and approve, change or skip each old label.
9. To remove an archived project for good, press **Delete…** and type its name.

## Good to know

- A project without a card is read-only. Press **Add a card** to fix that.
- Archived projects stay out of the list and pickers. Their links and to-dos keep working.
- Anyone who can edit a project can archive it. Only its Account Owner or the brain's Owner can delete it.
- Delete is final. Files that exist only on this Mac can't be recovered. Deleted to-dos can be brought back, and chats are kept.
- Update Cortex on every Mac before the first archive. Older apps can't see archived projects.
- On Home, **Working on** narrows your work to one project.
- Tidy-up changes teammates' to-dos too. Only one person should run it, straight after a sync.
- If the brain's skills are out of date, Projects is read-only until you update them.

## Try this

Open Projects, choose **Mine**, and open the first project. Press **To-dos** to see what is open.
