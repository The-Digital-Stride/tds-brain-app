### Brain

The Brain is the agency's shared folder of knowledge. It holds skills, to-dos and client notes.

### Cortex

Cortex is the app you are using. Its full name is TDS Cortex.

### client

A client is a customer of the agency, such as a heating company. Chats and to-dos can be linked to one.

### chat

A chat is a conversation with Claude. Each chat keeps its own history.

### Claude

Claude is the AI that works with you in Cortex. It reads the Brain and helps with your jobs.

### skill

A skill is a saved set of steps Claude follows, such as filing call notes.

### helper

A helper is a small Claude assistant that does one part of a job for a chat.

### level

A level is how hard Claude works on something. The levels are Quick, Standard and Deep.

### approval

An approval is a question Claude asks before it does something. You say yes or no.

### to-do

A to-do is a job someone needs to do. To-dos live in the Brain, so the team sees them.

### drawer

A drawer is a panel that slides out from the rail, such as the To-dos drawer.

### rail

The rail is the strip of buttons down the side of Cortex. It opens drawers and pages.

### Home

Home is the first screen. It has the message box and your pinned cards.

### pin

Starred to-dos, chats and files also show on Home, under headings that start with Pinned.

### favourite

A favourite is something you starred, such as a chat, to-do, skill or file. It shows at the top of its list.

### sync

A sync is when your copy of the Brain updates and shares its changes. Teammates see your changes after their Brain next syncs.

### owner

An owner is someone who looks after the Brain. Owners can change things other people can't.

### scout

A scout is a team member with some extra rights, such as setting skill levels.

### team member

A team member is anyone at TDS who uses the Brain.

### token

A token is a small piece of text that Claude reads or writes. Fewer tokens make chats faster and cheaper.

### page

A page is a document Claude makes for you to read in Cortex.

### Dock

The Dock is the row of app icons on your Mac. You can add Cortex to it.

### terminal

The terminal is a window where you type commands. You never need it for everyday work in Cortex.

### Settings

Settings is where you change how Cortex behaves and check that it is working. It has five tabs: **General**, **Claude**, **Skill levels**, **Health** and **About**.

### Help

Help is this guide to Cortex. Open it from the rail to read about the screen you are on.
