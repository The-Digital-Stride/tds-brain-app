version: 0.52.0
title: Ads shows people's names, the due count straight away, and Previous and Next
pages: [ad-manager, ads-command-centre]
---

**Ads Manager** and the other Ads tools now show people by name, such as "Reviewer: Pete Tyler", instead of "pete-tyler". In the proposal panel, **Previous** and **Next** move through the proposals waiting for a decision, then the approved ones.

The Workbench menu now shows how many **Ads Command Centre** tasks are due as soon as Cortex starts.

Names appear once the updated Ads skill is copied into the Brain. Until then, you see the same short names as before.
